The boss behind the vision

Purpose in
every step.

Chubath Koang Chuol is a community-minded entrepreneur dedicated to building opportunities and serving families with honesty. Through Sojourners Investment, he brings together trusted products, warm service, and a vision for a stronger local community.

His leadership is guided by a simple belief: meaningful businesses should create value for people and open doors for others.

A journey rooted in care

Built from a love
of everyday life.

What began as a small neighbourhood market grew from Chubath’s belief that good groceries should feel personal, affordable, and consistently dependable. He spent years learning from local shoppers, farmers, and suppliers, building relationships around trust and quality.

Today, Sojourners Investment carries that same spirit into every shelf, every conversation, and every choice we make for our community. We choose products with care, support local partners, and keep service warm, honest, and human.
